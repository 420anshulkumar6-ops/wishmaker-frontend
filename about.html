About WishMaker

WishMaker is a simple, mobile-first tool that lets anyone create beautiful, personalised video wishes for the moments that matter — birthdays, Raksha Bandhan, Diwali, anniversaries and more.

🎯 Our Mission

Sending love shouldn't need video-editing skills. We want every person to be able to create a magical, full-screen video wish in under 30 seconds — just a photo, a message and a song.

✨ What You Can Create

  • Birthday video wishes with name and photo
  • Raksha Bandhan greetings for your brother or sister
  • Diwali wishes full of lights and festive music
  • Anniversary wishes for couples

📱 Built for WhatsApp Status

Every wish is designed in vertical, full-screen format — perfect for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Stories and sharing as a direct link with family and friends.
