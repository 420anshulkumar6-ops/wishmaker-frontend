Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, what data WishMaker handles and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a wish, we store the details you provide: the theme you choose, the recipient's name (if entered), your message, the selected song, and the photo you upload. We do not collect your email, phone number, or any account information — no sign-up is required.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used only for one purpose: to generate and display your video wish page. Your photo is stored securely and shown only on the unique wish link that is created for you.

3. Photo Storage

Uploaded photos are stored in private storage and served only through your unique wish link. We do not use your photos for advertising, training, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

Wishes are shared by you, through a link you choose to send (e.g. on WhatsApp). Anyone with the link can view that wish. We never share or sell your data to third parties.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the site is used (page visits, device type). We do not use advertising trackers.

6. Data Deletion

If you want a wish and its photo removed, contact us with the wish link and we will delete it. Wishes may also be automatically cleaned up after a period of time.

7. Children's Privacy

WishMaker is a creation tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please supervise minors when uploading photos.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or deletion requests, please reach us through the Contact page.
